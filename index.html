The shop, in short

One bench, one maker per piece, no production line.

Bracket & Bevel takes on a small number of commissions a year — dining tables, cabinetry, and the odd stubborn repair job other shops won't touch. Timber is selected board by board, and every joint is cut before it's glued, never after.
