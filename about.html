About the shop

One bench. One maker per piece.

Bracket & Bevel started as a one-person repair shop and stayed small on purpose. Every piece that leaves is built by the same hands that met the client, chose the boards, and cut the joints.

How a commission runs

Four stages, no shortcuts
  • Stage 1

    Conversation

    We talk through the room, the use, and the budget before any wood is chosen. If the timeline or budget doesn't fit solid joinery, we say so early.

  • Stage 2

    Timber selection

    Boards are picked in person for grain and moisture content, then left to acclimate in the shop for at least two weeks before cutting begins.

  • Stage 3

    Joinery and build

    Mortise and tenon, dovetail, or draw-bore — whichever joint suits the load and the wood movement, cut by hand or on shop-built jigs.

  • Stage 4

    Finish and delivery

    Oil or wax finishes that can be renewed at home for decades, plus a walkthrough on care for the specific species used.

Materials

Three species, kept in stock and known well.

White oak, black walnut, and ash cover most commissions. Anything outside those three is sourced specifically for the job, and we'll always explain the tradeoffs before you commit to a species.

Ask about a specific wood
  • White OakTight grain, water-resistant, ages to a warm grey outdoors.
  • Black WalnutDense and dark, the most requested wood for dining tables.
  • AshFlexible under steam, used for bent chair backs and stair parts.
